About Us

Who we are

Geotechnical Engineering and Technology Laboratory (GETLAB) Limited is a Limited Liability Company offering testing services for a wide range of civil infrastructure development projects including geotechnical, geophysical exploration and laboratory testing services since 2014.

Vision

To be the principle provider of Geotechnical and Material testing services

Mission

To provide competitive, high quality and timely engineering solutions

Team

Our team is composed of highly competent and well-exposed technicians, geologists, and engineers with extensive experience in geotechnical investigations, materials testing, and quality assurance.

Equipment

Our laboratory is equipped with modern, calibrated instruments including triaxial and direct shear apparatus, consolidation equipment, unconfined compression machines, California Bearing Ratio (CBR) testers, and advanced soil classification tools.

For pavement and construction materials, we also maintain state-of-the-art asphalt testing equipment (Marshall stability, bitumen content analyzers, penetration and softening point testers) and concrete testing equipment (compression testing machines, slump cones, and curing tanks).

All equipment is routinely calibrated and maintained to ensure accurate, reliable, and internationally compliant test results.

Certification

GETLAB holds ISO/IEC 17025 Accreditation Certificate issued by KENAS - Kenya Accreditation Service and some of her personnel hold American Concrete Institute (ACl) Training Certificates.

GETLAB is also a member of the National Laboratory Association of South Africa and is also qualified on the National Supplier Database(NSD) for supply of goods, works and services in the oil and gas sector in Uganda under the Uganda Petroleum Authority.
