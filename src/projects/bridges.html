Geotechnical investigations for detailed design and supervision for accelerated growth corridor road and access infrastructure package 1: Corridor 5 (Mkanda–Linga) and Corridor 15 (Chikwawa–Euthini) in Malawi

As part of the US‑funded Transport and Land Compact (approx. US $350 million) launched in May 2024, the Accelerated Growth Corridor (AGC) aims to reduce transport costs and improve rural connectivity by upgrading key agricultural roads across Malawi.
In June 2023, the consulting firm LEA Associates South Asia was selected to carry out the detailed design and supervision for the project.

LEA Associates South Asia contracted GETLAB to conduct the geotechnical investigations at the bridge sections of the different roads to access the ground parameters to aid the design process.
