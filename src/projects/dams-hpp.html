Geotechnical and Materials Testing Services for Isimba HPP

The Isimba Hydroelectric Power Station is a 183.2 MW run-of-the-river plant on the Victoria Nile, around 4 km downstream of Simba Falls in Kamuli District (close to Kayunga), roughly 50 km from Jinja. The dam and associated structures include a concrete gravity dam, a rock‑fill embankment, spillways, powerhouse, switchyard, and a 42 km double-circuit 132 kV transmission line linking to Bujagali substation.

GETLAB was contracted to conduct the material testing during the construction to ensure quality and the necessary standards were met.
